World War II :: The deadliest conflict in human history, lasting from 1939 to 1945 and resulting in approximately 70-85 million deaths
Treaty of Versailles :: A treaty signed in 1919 after World War I that imposed harsh penalties on Germany, creating resentment and economic hardship
The Holocaust :: The systematic, state-sponsored persecution and murder of six million Jews by the Nazi regime
Operation Barbarossa :: Germany's attack on the Soviet Union in 1941, opening a massive new Eastern Front
D-Day :: The Allied invasion of Normandy, France on June 6, 1944, marking the beginning of the liberation of Western Europe
Island-hopping :: The strategy the United States used to move closer to Japan while bypassing heavily defended islands
Marshall Plan :: The reconstruction program used to rebuild Europe after World War II
Appeasement :: The policy by Britain and France that allowed aggressive nations to expand unchecked before World War II
Battle of Britain ;; Major aerial conflict from July to October 1940 in which the Royal Air Force defeated the German Luftwaffe
Battle of Stalingrad ;; Brutal Soviet victory from 1942 to 1943 that halted German expansion in the East
Battle of Midway ;; 1942 turning point in the Pacific where the U.S. Navy defeated the Japanese fleet
attack on Pearl Harbor ;; December 7, 1941; killed over 1,100 American service members and destroyed numerous ships and aircraft
United Nations ;; Founded in 1945 to promote international peace and security
Cold War ;; Emerged after WWII as tensions between the Soviet Union and Western powers grew
