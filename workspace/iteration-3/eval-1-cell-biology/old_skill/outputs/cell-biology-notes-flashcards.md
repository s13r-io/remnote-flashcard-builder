Cell :: The basic structural and functional unit of all living organisms composed of one or more cells
Prokaryotic cell :: A type of cell that lacks a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles
Eukaryotic cell :: A type of cell that contains a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles
Cell membrane :: A thin, flexible barrier that surrounds the cell and separates its interior from the external environment, also called the plasma membrane
Phospholipid bilayer :: The primary component of the cell membrane consisting of two layers of phospholipids with hydrophobic tails facing inward and hydrophilic heads facing outward
Mitochondria :: The powerhouse of the cell responsible for producing ATP through the process of cellular respiration
Nucleus :: The largest organelle in eukaryotic cells that contains the cell's genetic material, primarily DNA
Ribosomes :: Structures where proteins are synthesized by reading messenger RNA (mRNA) and translating the genetic code into chains of amino acids
Endoplasmic reticulum :: A network of membranous sacs and tubes involved in protein and lipid synthesis
Rough endoplasmic reticulum :: Endoplasmic reticulum with ribosomes attached, involved in protein synthesis
Smooth endoplasmic reticulum :: Endoplasmic reticulum without ribosomes, involved in lipid synthesis and detoxification
Golgi apparatus :: A membrane-bound organelle consisting of stacked, flattened sacs called cisternae that modifies, packages, and ships proteins and lipids
Lysosomes :: Small, round organelles filled with digestive enzymes that break down waste materials and cellular debris
Cytoskeleton :: A network of protein filaments that gives the cell its shape and enables movement
Microfilaments :: Protein filaments made of actin that are part of the cytoskeleton
Intermediate filaments :: Protein filaments made of various proteins that are part of the cytoskeleton
Microtubules :: Protein filaments made of tubulin that are part of the cytoskeleton
Mitosis :: A type of cell division that produces two genetically identical daughter cells used for growth and repair
Meiosis :: A type of cell division that produces four genetically diverse daughter cells with half the chromosome number, used for sexual reproduction
