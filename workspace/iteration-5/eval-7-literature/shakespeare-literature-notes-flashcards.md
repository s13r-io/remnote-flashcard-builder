# Shakespeare: Life, Works, and Legacy

## Who Was William Shakespeare?

- **William Shakespeare** :: English playwright, poet, and actor, widely regarded as the greatest writer in the English language
- Shakespeare was born in {{Stratford-upon-Avon}} in 1564.
- Shakespeare was born in {{1564}} and died in {{1616}}.
- **How many plays did Shakespeare write approximately?** >> 39
- **How many sonnets did Shakespeare write?** >> 154
- **Lord Chamberlain's Men** :: The playing company Shakespeare belonged to, later renamed the King's Men
- The Lord Chamberlain's Men were renamed the King's Men under {{King James I}}.
- **Globe Theatre** :: Famous open-air playhouse in London where Shakespeare's works were performed
- The Globe Theatre was built in {{1599}}.

## The Tragedies

- **What defines Shakespeare's tragedies?** >> The downfall of a noble protagonist due to a fatal flaw
- **Hamlet** :: Tragedy about the Prince of Denmark who seeks to avenge his father's murder by his uncle Claudius and struggles with indecision
- **Describe: manipulates Othello into believing Desdemona is unfaithful** << Iago
- **Who murdered Hamlet's father?** >> His uncle Claudius
- **What is Hamlet's fatal flaw?** >> Indecision
- **Macbeth** :: Tragedy about a Scottish general driven to murder King Duncan by ambition and the prophecies of three witches
- **What motivates Macbeth to murder King Duncan?** >> Ambition and the prophecies of three witches
- **Othello** :: Tragedy about a Moorish general manipulated by his jealous ensign Iago into believing his wife Desdemona is unfaithful
- **Which of Othello's officers manipulates him?** >>A) Iago B) Cassio C) Roderigo D) Brabantio
- **King Lear** :: Tragedy in which a king divides his kingdom among his three daughters and descends into madness after betrayal
- **Which daughters betray King Lear?** >> Goneril and Regan
- **Describe: loyal daughter rejected by King Lear** << Cordelia

## The Sonnets

- Shakespeare published his 154 sonnets in {{1609}}.
- **Shakespearean sonnet form** :: Three quatrains and a final couplet, with the rhyme scheme ABAB CDCD EFEF GG
- **Sonnets 1-126** ;; Addressed to a young man, often called the "Fair Youth," urging him to marry and preserve his beauty through children
- **Sonnets 127-152** ;; Addressed to a mysterious woman known as the "Dark Lady"
- **Fair Youth** <> **Sonnets 1-126**
- **Dark Lady** <> **Sonnets 127-152**
- **Sonnet 18** ;; Begins "Shall I compare thee to a summer's day?", among the most famous poems in the English language
- **What is the rhyme scheme of a Shakespearean sonnet?** >> ABAB CDCD EFEF GG
