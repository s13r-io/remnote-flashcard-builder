# World War II Flashcards

## When did World War II begin?
- **Front:** When did World War II begin?
- **Back:** September 1, 1939, when Nazi Germany invaded Poland

## Who led Nazi Germany during World War II?
- **Front:** Who led Nazi Germany during World War II?
- **Back:** Adolf Hitler

## When did Britain and France declare war on Germany?
- **Front:** When did Britain and France declare war on Germany?
- **Back:** September 3, 1939 (two days after Germany invaded Poland)

## What was the estimated death toll of World War II?
- **Front:** What was the estimated death toll of World War II?
- **Back:** Approximately 70-85 million deaths

## What treaty imposed harsh penalties on Germany after World War I?
- **Front:** What treaty imposed harsh penalties on Germany after World War I?
- **Back:** The Treaty of Versailles (1919)

## Who were the three main authoritarian leaders who created militaristic regimes?
- **Front:** Who were the three main authoritarian leaders who created militaristic regimes bent on expansion?
- **Back:** Adolf Hitler (Germany), Benito Mussolini (Italy), and Hideki Tojo (Japan)

## When did Germany conquer France?
- **Front:** When did Germany conquer France?
- **Back:** By June 1940

## Who was the Prime Minister of Britain during World War II?
- **Front:** Who was the Prime Minister of Britain during World War II?
- **Back:** Winston Churchill

## What was the Battle of Britain?
- **Front:** What was the Battle of Britain?
- **Back:** A major aerial conflict (July-October 1940) in which the Royal Air Force defeated the German Luftwaffe, preventing a German invasion of Britain

## When did Germany attack the Soviet Union?
- **Front:** When did Germany attack the Soviet Union and what was the operation called?
- **Back:** 1941, in Operation Barbarossa

## When did the United States enter World War II?
- **Front:** When did the United States enter World War II?
- **Back:** December 7, 1941, following the Japanese attack on Pearl Harbor

## How many American service members were killed in the Pearl Harbor attack?
- **Front:** How many American service members were killed in the Pearl Harbor attack?
- **Back:** Over 1,100

## What was the strategy used by the United States in the Pacific Theater?
- **Front:** What was the strategy used by the United States in the Pacific Theater?
- **Back:** Island-hopping - moving closer to Japan while bypassing heavily defended islands

## What was the Holocaust?
- **Front:** What was the Holocaust?
- **Back:** The systematic, state-sponsored persecution and murder of six million Jews by the Nazi regime, along with millions of others including Roma, disabled people, political prisoners, and Soviet prisoners of war

## What was the Battle of Stalingrad?
- **Front:** What was the Battle of Stalingrad?
- **Back:** A brutal Soviet victory (1942-1943) that halted German expansion in the East

## What was the significance of the Battle of Midway?
- **Front:** What was the significance of the Battle of Midway?
- **Back:** It was a turning point in the Pacific War (1942) where the U.S. Navy defeated the Japanese fleet

## What was D-Day?
- **Front:** What was D-Day?
- **Back:** June 6, 1944 - the Allied invasion of Normandy in France, marking the beginning of the liberation of Western Europe

## When did Germany surrender in World War II?
- **Front:** When did Germany surrender in World War II?
- **Back:** May 7, 1945, following Hitler's suicide and the Soviet capture of Berlin

## When was the atomic bomb dropped on Hiroshima?
- **Front:** When was the atomic bomb dropped on Hiroshima?
- **Back:** August 6, 1945

## When was the atomic bomb dropped on Nagasaki?
- **Front:** When was the atomic bomb dropped on Nagasaki?
- **Back:** August 9, 1945

## When did Japan formally surrender?
- **Front:** When did Japan formally surrender?
- **Back:** August 15, 1945

## Who was Franklin D. Roosevelt?
- **Front:** Who was Franklin D. Roosevelt?
- **Back:** President of the United States during World War II

## Who was Josef Stalin?
- **Front:** Who was Josef Stalin?
- **Back:** Leader of the Soviet Union during World War II

## Who was Emperor Hirohito?
- **Front:** Who was Emperor Hirohito?
- **Back:** Emperor of Japan during World War II

## What was the Marshall Plan?
- **Front:** What was the Marshall Plan?
- **Back:** A reconstruction program for Europe after World War II

## When was the United Nations founded?
- **Front:** When was the United Nations founded?
- **Back:** 1945

## What was the purpose of the United Nations?
- **Front:** What was the purpose of the United Nations?
- **Back:** To promote international peace and security

## What conflicts emerged after World War II?
- **Front:** What major conflict emerged as a result of World War II?
- **Back:** The Cold War, which emerged as tensions between the Soviet Union and the Western powers grew

## What happened to Germany and Japan after World War II?
- **Front:** What happened to Germany and Japan after World War II?
- **Back:** Both nations were occupied and demilitarized

## What process did World War II accelerate regarding colonial empires?
- **Front:** What process did World War II accelerate regarding colonial empires?
- **Back:** Decolonization in Asia and Africa, as weakened European powers could no longer maintain their empires

## What were the three main causes of World War II?
- **Front:** What were the three main causes of World War II?
- **Back:** 1) The Treaty of Versailles imposing harsh penalties on Germany (1919), 2) Economic depression in the 1930s causing unemployment and social unrest, 3) The rise of authoritarian militaristic leaders (Hitler, Mussolini, Tojo)

## What was the primary conflict zone of World War II?
- **Front:** What was the primary conflict zone of World War II?
- **Back:** The European Theater

## What major battles occurred in the Pacific Theater?
- **Front:** What major battles occurred in the Pacific Theater?
- **Back:** Midway, Guadalcanal, Iwo Jima, and Okinawa

## How long did World War II last?
- **Front:** How long did World War II last?
- **Back:** From 1939 to 1945 (6 years)

## What was the consequence of appeasement by Britain and France?
- **Front:** What was the consequence of appeasement by Britain and France?
- **Back:** It allowed aggressive nations to expand unchecked before the war began
